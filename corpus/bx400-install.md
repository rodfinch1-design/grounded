# Clearances

Maintain 24 inches of service clearance at the front of the BX-400 and 6 inches
at the sides. The unit must not be installed in an unconditioned attic where
ambient exceeds 130 F.

[[page:2]]
# Condensate

The primary drain is 3/4 inch PVC. A secondary drain pan with a float switch is
required for any installation above a finished ceiling.

Trap depth must be at least 2 inches to prevent air pull-through on negative
pressure applications.

[[page:3]]
# Electrical

The BX-400 requires a dedicated 15 amp circuit. Minimum circuit ampacity is
11.2 amps. Use 14 AWG copper minimum for runs under 50 feet.
