# Scope

This manual covers service of the AX-200 condensing unit, serial range
AX2-0400 and above. Units below AX2-0400 are covered by revision 1.0.

[[page:2]]
# Flange Bolt Torque

Torque the compressor flange bolts to 35 ft-lb in a star pattern. Revision 2.1
raised this figure from the value published in revision 1.0 following field
reports of gasket weep at the lower torque.

Re-torque after the first 50 hours of operation. Do not exceed 40 ft-lb; the
flange casting will crack above that figure.

[[page:3]]
# Refrigerant Charge

The AX-200 ships with a factory charge of 4.2 lb of R-410A. Field charge
adjustment must be made by weight, not by pressure, in ambient conditions below
60 F.

Subcooling target at design conditions is 10 F.

[[page:4]]
# Error Codes

Code E-11 indicates a high-pressure lockout. Reset by cycling disconnect power
for 30 seconds. If E-11 repeats within one hour, check condenser airflow before
replacing the pressure switch.

Code E-27 indicates loss of communication with the outdoor board. Verify the
4-wire harness at connector P3 before replacing either board.

[[page:5]]
# Warranty

The sealed system carries a 10 year limited warranty on parts when the unit is
registered within 90 days of installation. Labour is not covered.
