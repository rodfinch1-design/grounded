# Scope

This manual covers service of the AX-200 condensing unit, serial range
AX2-0001 through AX2-0399.

[[page:2]]
# Flange Bolt Torque

Torque the compressor flange bolts to 28 ft-lb in a star pattern.

[[page:3]]
# Refrigerant Charge

The AX-200 ships with a factory charge of 3.8 lb of R-410A.
