# When I Grow Up

*A two-voice conversation. ADULT is warm, curious, a little amused. KID is about six or seven, and every answer is the best idea anyone has ever had.*

*Stage directions in italics are performance notes. `generate.py` turns them into ElevenLabs v3 audio tags, so they double as emotional direction for the voices.*

---

**ADULT:** *(warm)* So, buddy. Big question. What do you wanna be when you grow up?

**KID:** *(excited)* A football player!

**ADULT:** *(amused)* A football player. Okay. Tell me what you'd do as a football player.

**KID:** *(excited)* Okay, so. I would get the ball, and I would run. Like, SO fast. Faster than everybody. And the other guys would try to get me, but they can't, 'cause I'm too fast. And then I'd jump over one guy... and then I do a spin... and then... *(shouts)* TOUCHDOWN! *(excited)* And everybody's screaming my name. And then I do my dance.

**ADULT:** *(curious)* You have a dance?

**KID:** *(matter-of-fact)* I have like four dances.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* Four dances. Okay. What about a basketball player?

**KID:** *(gasps)* Ooh. Yeah. I could do that. I could do basketball.

**ADULT:** Tell me what you'd do.

**KID:** *(excited)* So I get the ball. And I'm dribbling it, like, between my legs, behind my back, and the other guy's like, "Where'd the ball go?" And I'm like, "It's right here!" And then I jump... *(dramatic)* and I'm up there for a long time. Like, a really long time. Like, I could eat a snack up there. *(excited)* And then I dunk it so hard the hoop breaks, and they have to get a new one.

**ADULT:** *(amused)* They have to get a whole new hoop.

**KID:** *(serious)* Every game.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* That's a lot of hoops. Okay. What about a doctor?

**KID:** *(thoughtful)* A doctor... *(excited)* Yeah! A doctor's cool. I could be a doctor.

**ADULT:** *(curious)* What kind of doctor?

**KID:** *(thoughtful)* Um. A doctor for... everybody. Like if you have a broken arm, or a cold, or you have a boo-boo, or a shark bit you.

**ADULT:** *(amused)* If a shark bit you.

**KID:** *(confident)* Yeah, I'd fix that. I'd have a big white coat with a lot of pockets. And I'd have the thing you put in your ears, and I'd listen to your heart and go, *(serious)* "Hmm. Hmm. Okay." *(excited)* And then I'd give you a shot, but it doesn't even hurt, because I do it super fast. And then I give you a sticker. And a lollipop. *(thoughtful)* Actually, two lollipops.

**ADULT:** *(warm)* Two lollipops. From Doctor... what's your doctor name?

**KID:** *(proud)* Doctor Awesome.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* Doctor Awesome. Okay. What about a wrestler? Like a pro wrestler?

**KID:** *(gasps)* I could do that. *(excited)* I could TOTALLY do a wrestler.

**ADULT:** *(amused)* Tell me. Tell me what you'd do.

**KID:** *(excited)* Okay okay okay. So the lights go off. *(whispers)* And my music comes on, and it's like, *(shouts)* BOOM. BOOM. BOOM. And there's fire! *(excited)* And I come out and everybody's like, "AHHHH!" And my name is... *(dramatic)* The Thunder Kid. *(excited)* And I got a cape. And I get in the ring, and the bad guy's like, "You're too small!" And I'm like, *(serious)* "That's what YOU think." *(excited)* And I pick him up, and I spin him around like ten times, and I throw him. And then I jump off the top rope and... *(shouts)* BAM. *(excited)* One, two, three. And I get the belt.

**ADULT:** *(impressed)* Wow.

**KID:** *(serious)* It's a really big belt.

**ADULT:** *(curious)* What about a firefighter?

**KID:** *(thoughtful)* ...Yeah! *(excited)* I'd slide down the pole. And I'd drive the truck. Nobody else gets to drive it. I drive it. And I'd go *(shouts)* "WEE-oo, WEE-oo." *(matter-of-fact)* That's the siren. I do it myself. *(excited)* And there's a cat in a tree, and a house on fire, and I'd get the cat first, 'cause the cat's scared. And then I spray the fire. And the water's so strong it goes all the way up to the clouds, and it makes it rain, and the rain puts out the fire. *(proud)* That's how I do it.

**ADULT:** *(warm)* That's smart. What about an astronaut?

**KID:** *(whispers)* Space. Yeah. I'd go to space. *(excited)* I'd go to the moon, and I'd jump really high, 'cause there's no... *(thoughtful)* gravity? *(excited)* And I'd bounce. And I'd find an alien, and he's actually nice, and he's my best friend, and his name is Greg. *(warm)* And I'd bring back a moon rock for you. And one for Mom. *(giggles)* And a little one for the dog.

**ADULT:** *(amused)* Okay. So. Football player. Basketball player. Doctor. Wrestler. Firefighter. Astronaut. That's a lot of jobs, buddy. You're gonna have to pick one.

**KID:** *(confused)* ...Why?

**ADULT:** *(hesitant)* I mean...

**KID:** *(matter-of-fact)* I'll just do all of 'em. Monday, I do football. Tuesday, basketball. Wednesday's doctor day. Thursday, wrestling. Friday, fire truck. *(whispers)* And then on the weekend... space.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* When do you sleep?

**KID:** *(matter-of-fact)* In space. It's dark there anyway.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* You know what? *(warm)* I think you're gonna be just fine.

**KID:** *(cheerful)* I know. Can we have a snack now?

*End.*
