# When I Grow Up (Short)

*The under-60-second cut for a YouTube Short. Three jobs instead of six, and the punchline lands faster.*

---

**ADULT:** *(warm)* Buddy. What do you wanna be when you grow up?

**KID:** *(excited)* A football player!

**ADULT:** *(curious)* What do you do as a football player?

**KID:** *(excited)* I get the ball, I run SO fast, I jump over a guy, and... *(shouts)* TOUCHDOWN! *(excited)* Then I do my dance.

**ADULT:** *(amused)* What about a doctor?

**KID:** *(gasps)* Ooh. *(excited)* If a shark bites you, I fix it. I listen to your heart, *(serious)* "Hmm. Okay." *(excited)* Then two lollipops.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* A pro wrestler?

**KID:** *(excited)* Lights go off. BOOM, BOOM, BOOM. I'm The Thunder Kid! I spin him ten times, I throw him, and... *(shouts)* BAM! *(excited)* One, two, three. I get the belt.

**ADULT:** *(amused)* That's a lot of jobs, buddy. You gotta pick one.

**KID:** *(confused)* ...Why? *(deadpan)* Monday, football. Tuesday, doctor. Wednesday, wrestling.

**ADULT:** *(laughs)* When do you sleep?

**KID:** *(deadpan)* Thursday.

---

*End.*
